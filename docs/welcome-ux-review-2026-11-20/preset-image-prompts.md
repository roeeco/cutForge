# Preset and mission pictures: spec and prompts (2026-11-21)

Today every preset and mission is shown as a live render of its design: correct, but bare (an empty tan box). These pictures show **what each one is for and how it opens**: the contents, a flap half open, a lid lifted. They go where a choice is made:
- the sentence's first-word list (thumbnails);
- *Follow a mission* in the composer;
- Guided's gallery.

The composer's big preview stays live, because it follows the sizes and the material.

## What I need

- **30 images**, saved to `public/presets/` with the file names below (`<name>.png` or `.jpg`). I'll compress them and wire them in.
- **1200 × 800 px (3:2)**, background a flat **#EEF1F5** to the edges, the object centred and filling about 70% of the frame. Thumbnails are as small as 96 × 64, so the shape must read at that size.
- No text, no logos, no people, no hands.
- Make them in batches of the same group (boxes, cartons, flat pieces, missions), so the light and camera match within a row.

**Style lock** (end every prompt with it):

```
Clean product render, 3/4 view from about 30° above, soft studio light from the upper left, gentle contact shadow, on a perfectly flat plain #EEF1F5 background, the object centred and filling about 70% of the frame.
```
